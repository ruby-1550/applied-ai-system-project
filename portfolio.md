## Reflection (what this says about me as an AI engineer)

This project shows that I build AI systems that are **observable, testable, and honest about their limits**. Instead of treating “AI” as a black box, I designed an end-to-end workflow that combines retrieval, structured reasoning, and guardrails, then validated it with an evaluation harness and unit tests. I care about reliability and responsible design: the system produces traceable explanations, documents intended/non‑intended use, and makes it easy for others to reproduce results from a clean setup.
