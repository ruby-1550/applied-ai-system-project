Comparison Notes for Tested Profiles

- High-Energy Pop vs Chill Lofi: The pop profile pushes fast, upbeat tracks with higher energy and danceability, while the lofi profile shifts toward low-energy, higher-acoustic songs with slower tempos.
- High-Energy Pop vs Deep Intense Rock: Both prefer high energy, but the rock profile pulls in harsher moods and slightly lower valence, which makes the results feel more aggressive.
- High-Energy Pop vs Conflicting High-Energy Sad: The conflicting profile still attracts high-energy songs, but lower valence makes some recommendations feel emotionally off.
- Chill Lofi vs Deep Intense Rock: These profiles sit at opposite ends of energy and tempo, so the output flips from calm, acoustic tracks to fast, intense tracks.
- Chill Lofi vs Conflicting High-Energy Sad: The lofi profile favors calm and acoustic tracks, while the conflicting profile leans toward energy despite sad mood, creating a mix that feels less consistent.
- Deep Intense Rock vs Conflicting High-Energy Sad: Both like high energy, but the sad profile’s low valence and different genre choices shift the list away from aggressive rock.
